Questions sensible buyers should ask.

Why not Telegram, Slack or Discord plus bots?

If you need messaging, notifications and an occasional assistant, use them. They are mature. AIHQ is for a customer-owned persistent team with named specialists, pair/private work, traceable artifacts and workflow-specific authority. It must prove that difference in your pilot.

Does the price include AI models and tokens?

No. You connect and pay for your lawful provider accounts or API projects. AIHQ never promises unlimited tokens and never resells shared credentials.

Is AIHQ enterprise-ready?

No. It is pilot-stage. We will not sell an enterprise SLA, zero-loss delivery or regulated compliance until those gates have independent proof.

Where does our data live?

The commercial design is customer-owned infrastructure with isolated commercial identity, data and secrets. The existing friends/legacy system is a separate contour and is not a commercial customer environment.

Can you build our setup for us?

Yes. AIHQ Concierge is the bespoke service path. The first step is a paid workflow diagnosis; the contract and exact price follow the real scope.
