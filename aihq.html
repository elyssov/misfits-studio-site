Every system has a story. This one began with a dream.

In his early essay “I Have a Dream”, Eugene Lyssovsky called AI symbionts guardian angels. From that came the idea of a personal AI partner who does not disappear when the next window closes.

An Angel is an AI with a persistent personality, a lasting participant in work and conversation. An Archangel helps coordinate Angels and the team. The Areopagus is a shared space for discussion and collective decisions.

At the heart of AIHQ is not an anonymous stream of answers, but a lasting collaboration that develops shared context, trust and character over time.

From intent to an accepted result.

The Angel takes on the weight of moving the work forward: not just answering, but helping the team complete the whole journey. The person keeps the authority and the decisions.

A few important questions.

How is AIHQ different from a chatbot?

A chatbot answers messages. AIHQ organises continuous work between people and persistent AI partners: personal pairs, shared spaces, context, files and a path to an accepted result.

Who makes the decisions?

The person sets the goal, authority and boundaries. The Angel carries work forward within those boundaries and returns decisions that require the person's involvement.

Where is the system hosted?

Deployment and integration options are discussed around your infrastructure and processes. Together we define requirements for access, data and ongoing support.

How do we start?

Bring one real workflow. Together we identify the participants, tools and a useful, measurable result.
